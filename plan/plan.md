# Outdooroots — Platform Direction and Product Logic Proposal

## Purpose
Reorient the existing Chile Travel Builder into Outdooroots’ digital storefront and trip-design workspace: personalized outdoor adventures connecting travelers with Chile’s nature, culture, and local communities.

The platform should help visitors find a suitable starting point, explain what they want, understand an honest estimate, and send the team an actionable inquiry. It should help the team turn those inquiries into reviewed proposals—not imply that assembling a package reserves guides, transport, or accommodation.

This is a proposal for the next revision, not a statement that the changes are complete. The earlier feature work was interrupted while still in progress; the booking service has an unfinished dependency and should not be treated as ready for use. Completing that work and restoring the existing booking journey are included in this revision.

The reports support personalization, local knowledge, adventure, community, safety, better lead handling, and three signature packages. Luxury is an optional segment, not the identity of every trip.

Their forecasts are planning hypotheses, not pricing instructions or established results. For example, the comprehensive report’s 2030 revenue of CLP 300 million, cost of goods of CLP 150 million, and operating expenses of CLP 100 million do not support the stated CLP 150 million net profit. These projections will not become automated margin rules or public claims.

## Decisions carried forward
- Brand: **Outdooroots**, with “Aventura · Vida · Naturaleza” and “En un nuevo viaje” informing the copy.
- Retain the premium, light-and-airy editorial presentation, elegant serif headings, deep blue accents, destination photography, and restrained motion. Premium presentation does not mean luxury-only products.
- Preserve the working trip-builder and protected team-console concepts, along with previously stored inquiries.
- Keep Patagonia, Atacama, Santiago & Central Chile, Easter Island, and the Lake District as the five destination chapters.
- Complete the previously requested visual itinerary timeline and downloadable, beautifully typeset one-page PDF as part of this direction.
- **No email alerts or automated emails.** This explicitly supersedes the reports’ email-automation recommendations.
- Keep EUR as the default visitor-facing estimate currency. Do not silently switch the existing experience to USD or CLP.

## Audience and navigation
### Primary: international adventure travelers
Lead with locally designed experiences, nature, cultural connection, and an appropriate level of challenge. Accommodation is one part of the journey rather than the main selling point.

### Secondary: corporate and private groups
Provide a clear “Plan a group experience” entry point. Capture organization, group size, dates, goals, budget, and planning requirements. Large groups receive a tailored inquiry workflow rather than forced consumer pricing or the current small-party limit.

### Also supported: domestic travelers
Make short adventures, weekends, and family preferences discoverable. Offer English and Spanish for the visitor journey, with English remaining the default. EUR remains the estimate currency in both languages during this revision and is clearly identified.

Photography and workation needs become optional trip interests rather than separate marketplaces or five competing primary funnels. Dedicated products for these niches can follow once the core offer is established.

## Visitor experience
### Three ways to start
1. **Explore a signature journey:** choose a curated starting itinerary and personalize it.
2. **Design a personal adventure:** begin with interests and practical preferences, then choose regions and experiences.
3. **Plan a group experience:** send a group-specific brief for the team to develop.

The reports call for three signature packages but do not name or operationally validate them. Proposed starting concepts are:
- Patagonia on Foot — trekking, landscapes, and lodge or locally hosted stays
- Atacama Under the Stars — desert exploration, local culture, and astronomy
- Lakes, Forests & Local Life — lakeside activities, forest experiences, and regional culture

These are editable itinerary concepts, not claims of scheduled departures, contracted suppliers, or guaranteed prices. Day adventures and custom trips remain available; visitors are not forced into multi-region packages.

### A short, progressive brief
Collect the information needed to make recommendations useful:
- Personal trip or group experience
- Dates or flexible travel window, duration, and party composition
- Budget per person or total group budget, explicitly distinguished
- Interests, preferred pace, outdoor experience level, and accommodation comfort
- Optional mobility or accessibility requirements and practical preferences

Keep contact details until the visitor is ready to request a proposal. Avoid mandatory medical histories, marketing enrollment, or unnecessary personal data.

### Recommendations, not an unexplained catalog
Show why a suggested trip or experience fits the visitor’s stated interests, duration, pace, and budget. Keep choices editable. Distinguish a relevant suggestion from confirmed availability.

## Trip-design logic
- Let travelers arrange destination order. Show the effect of their choices on days, nights, experiences, and estimated cost.
- Account for full-day and half-day activities without placing more than a day’s activity into one day.
- Include free time and explicitly identify onward-travel or transfer time that still needs planning.
- Treat distant regions and Easter Island as journeys requiring transport review. Do not present transfers or flights as instantaneous, included, or confirmed.
- Count overnight stays across the complete trip. Assign each night to a destination or an explicitly unresolved transit segment rather than dropping an accommodation night at every chapter boundary.
- Compare the itinerary’s duration with the preferred date range. Show inconsistencies without silently changing the traveler’s dates.
- Surface season, difficulty, age suitability, access, and acclimatization considerations only where the team has supplied reliable guidance. Unknown conditions remain “team review required,” not invented safety assurances.
- Show experiences that do not fit as unscheduled, never silently omit them or portray an overloaded day as feasible.
- Allow an incomplete or ambitious brief to be sent for review, while making its unresolved items explicit. A request is not a confirmed itinerary.

## Pricing and commercial integrity
### Visitor-facing estimates
- Show both the trip total and per-person amount, including the number of travelers used in the calculation.
- Separate accommodation, experiences, guide or transport charges, and any other included costs. State inclusions and exclusions.
- Identify the charging basis: per person, per group, per room, per night, or per day. Do not multiply a shared guide, vehicle, or room cost by every traveler.
- Remove unexplained base charges and avoid charging again for items already included in a package or lodge arrangement.
- Distinguish an indicative estimate from a team-reviewed quote and from a confirmed booking.
- Existing sample rates remain explicitly illustrative until the team approves real commercial rates. Missing prices appear as “quote required”; they must not appear as free or guaranteed.

### Team-controlled commercial inputs
- Maintain approved underlying costs in CLP, the business currency used in the reports.
- Display EUR using an explicitly recorded, dated exchange rate approved by the team. No live foreign-exchange feed or invented default exchange rate is included.
- Allow the team to set selling prices or margins from real costs. Keep supplier costs and margins private.
- Apply tax treatment and service fees only when configured and identified by the team. Do not assume the existing blanket 19% charge applies to every item.
- Reviewed quotes show a validity date and identify outstanding availability or cost checks. The reports’ broad pricing ranges and profit targets are not substitutes for supplier rates.

## Timeline and PDF
### Visual itinerary
Provide a mobile-friendly, day-by-day view with destination chapters, selected experiences, downtime, lodging nights, dates when known, and unresolved transfer or capacity issues. It must reflect the current composition rather than a separate decorative itinerary.

### One-page PDF
Create a branded Outdooroots itinerary summary containing:
- Package name, traveler count, dates or flexibility, and trip duration
- Destination day ranges, experience highlights, and proposed stays
- EUR estimate or reviewed quote, inclusions, exclusions, and quote validity where applicable
- Important outstanding planning items and a clear “proposal, not reservation” label

The PDF is a one-page summary, including for long journeys; it is not a promise to fit every expanded day detail onto one readable page. The full daily view remains on the platform. Visitors can download their composition before submitting contact details. The team can produce a reviewed version without exposing internal costs or notes.

## Inquiry and team workflow
Replace booking-certainty language with “Request my adventure proposal” or its Spanish equivalent.

After submission, show an on-screen reference and explain that the Outdooroots team will review the request. Do not promise an unsupported response time or imply that an email has been sent.

The team console becomes a practical inquiry pipeline:
- New → Contacted → Proposal sent → Confirmed, with Lost and Archived outcomes
- Search and filter by stage, dates, destination, and personal or group inquiry
- View the traveler’s brief, itinerary, estimate, source, and unresolved questions together
- Add internal notes, a next action, and a follow-up date
- Review commercial details and prepare a customer-facing PDF proposal
- Record why an inquiry was lost or needs further review

Existing requests and their original details must remain available. Internal notes, costs, and margin information must not enter visitor-facing exports.

## Business visibility
Show operational measures grounded in actual platform activity:
- Inquiry volume and stage distribution
- Overdue follow-ups and time to first contact
- Proposal-to-confirmation conversion
- Requested destinations, seasons, budgets, and trip types
- Inquiry source where provided or captured from a referral link
- Confirmed quote value, explicitly distinct from collected revenue

Do not display invented CAC, ROI, customer satisfaction, sustainability impact, or profitability figures without the supporting records. The reports’ targets are not current dashboard results.

## Trust and publication rules
- Present local culture and responsible travel as the offer’s direction, without fabricating community partnerships or measurable impact.
- Use “Sernatur registration #81017” publicly only after the registration and relevant scope are verified. Registration is not a blanket certification of all activities or sustainability practices.
- Do not publish Green Globe, ISO, other future certifications, invented reviews, customer counts, or safety guarantees as current achievements.
- Treat named lodges as preferences subject to review, not evidence of commercial partnerships or inventory.
- Use the Outdooroots wordmark until an approved logo asset is available; do not invent an official mountain-and-fox logo.
- Replace placeholder contact details only with confirmed business contacts. Do not direct inquiries to guessed addresses or telephone numbers.

## Assumptions worth challenging
- Outdooroots is the public brand replacing “Chile Travel Builder,” rather than a separate operator listed inside it.
- International adventure travelers are the primary audience, with corporate/group travel second, following the comprehensive report’s priority table despite inconsistent narrative ordering elsewhere.
- The business remains a Chile-based operator serving domestic and international travelers, not a marketplace or an operator of overseas tours.
- The current phase is **inquiry and reviewed proposal**, not instant booking or guaranteed inventory.
- English and Spanish are included now; EUR remains the default displayed currency. A visitor currency selector is deferred.
- All five destination chapters remain discoverable, but unverified offerings are available by inquiry rather than presented as confirmed operating capacity.
- Real supplier costs, rates, season rules, verified qualifications, business contacts, and policy wording require operator approval. Until then, the platform distinguishes examples and unknowns instead of fabricating them.

## Explicitly outside this revision
- Email alerts, confirmation emails, SMS, and automated marketing messages
- Payment collection, deposits, refunds, or a payment-provider integration
- Live lodge, guide, flight, or transport inventory and instant reservations
- An external CRM, booking marketplace, exchange-rate provider, or messaging integration
- Loyalty schemes, automated dynamic pricing, a supplier portal, and a native mobile app
- Guaranteed sustainability metrics, certifications, or business results

Later additions can include payment deposits after reviewed quotes, approved departure calendars, referral rewards, traveler draft saving and sharing, and specialized photography or workation products. Email automation remains excluded unless explicitly requested again.
